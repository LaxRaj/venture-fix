# New ideas (non video-editing, non lead-gen) - $0 capital, solo, AI-assisted

Method: one standard WebSearch pass per offer, 2026-11-06. Prices come almost entirely from vendor and agency marketing pages, so treat them as asking prices, not paid prices. Delivery hours per account, time to first dollar, and the "mine" pool numbers are MY ESTIMATES. I found no operator-reported revenue (Reddit/forum) for any offer, so every "demand" entry below is "vendors sell it", not "buyers verified paying". No outreach was done. Nothing here is a revenue forecast.

## Ranking
By revenue per delivery hour (est., after AI assist): 1 Newsletter ghostwriting at the $1,200-2,500 tier (~$150-200), 2 AI receptionist setup (~$100-165), 3 GBP/review management (~$80-100), 4 Bookkeeping catch-up (~$80-120 as a project, ~$50-80 on retainer), 5 Podcast show notes (~$150+ but only $240-400/mo per show and price-eroding), 6 Invoice-to-spreadsheet (~$20-40), 7 Catalog cleanup (~$25-50), 8 Short-form content (overlaps video lane, not scored).

By fastest first dollar (est.): 1 Bookkeeping catch-up (~1-2 wks), 2 GBP review management (~1-2 wks), 3 Podcast show notes (~1-2 wks), 4 AI receptionist (~2-3 wks), 5 Catalog cleanup (days, but pennies), 6 Newsletter (~3-6 wks).

By pool of 5,000+ small buyers, measurable: Bookkeeping (36.2M US small businesses), GBP (any local business), Shopify catalogs (2.5-3.5M live stores) and trades (100k+, unclear count) clear it. Newsletter and podcast pools are real but not cleanly countable.

Best combined (not best on any single axis): 1 Bookkeeping catch-up/cleanup, 2 GBP + review response, 3 AI receptionist for trades. Newsletter has the best margin but the slowest, trust-heavy sale.

## 1. Catch-up bookkeeping / QuickBooks cleanup (project, then retainer)
- **Buyer:** Owner of a sole proprietorship or small LLC (restaurants, contractors, consultants) with 6-24 months of unreconciled books, often before tax time or a loan.
- **Pays today:** Catch-up $500-$3,000, cleanup $300-$1,200 ([NY bookkeeper listing via search](https://business.google.com/v/bookkeeper-ms-24/08154058048440861725/9909/_/lp/6361253617323273080/)); typical 6-12 month backlog $800-$2,500 ([SDO CPA guide](https://www.sdocpa.com/catch-up-bookkeeping-cost-guide/)); ongoing $150-$400/mo ([RemoteBooks](https://www.remotebooksonline.com/blog/how-much-do-bookkeeping-services-cost-2025-pricing-guide)); QuickBooks Live cleanup reportedly starts at $500 one-time ([ZoftwareHub](https://zoftwarehub.com/products/quickbooks-live/pricing)).
- **Hours per account per month (mine):** Cleanup 8-20 hrs one-time with AI categorisation; ongoing 3-5 hrs/mo. About $80-120/hr on projects, $50-80/hr on retainers.
- **Pool:** 36,207,130 US small businesses, of which roughly 28.5M are nonemployer (2024 figure, secondary source) ([SBA Advocacy 2026](https://advocacy.sba.gov/2026/02/03/advocacy-releases-frequently-asked-questions-about-small-businesses-2026/), [2024 FAQ mirror](https://colemanreport.com/frequently-asked-questions-about-small-businesses)). Count free: SBA state tables, Census CBP.
- **Free reach channel:** Local Facebook/Nextdoor groups, Reddit r/smallbusiness answers, tax-season "my books are a mess" posts, Intuit ProAdvisor free directory listing (verify requirements).
- **Blockers:** No state licence for bookkeeping in most states; do not use the CPA title ([summary sources](https://smallbusiness.chron.com/bookkeepers-need-licensed-18481.html)). Oregon had a 2009 bill (SB 865) creating a licence; I could not confirm current status, so check. Avoid tax returns, payroll and sales-tax filings (separate registrations). Real risk is handling client financial data with no insurance.
- **First dollar (mine):** 1-2 weeks.
- **Demand evidence:** Many firms publish flat catch-up pricing and Intuit sells it, so the product exists. Hype check: all prices are from sellers; no buyer-side proof.

## 2. Google Business Profile management + review replies (local SEO-lite)
- **Buyer:** Single-location service businesses (dentists, salons, HVAC, restaurants).
- **Pays today:** $125-$500 per location per month, setup $300-$500 ([Merchynt](https://merchynt.com/post/google-my-business-management-pricing)); $249/mo package covering reviews, Q&A, photos ([Niagara](https://niagarastandsout.ca/products/google-business-profile-management-service)); one agency estimates 4-6 hrs/mo of work ([Colorado Web Impressions](https://coloradowebimpressions.com/answers/how-much-does-google-business-profile-management-cost)). Software alternatives $20-$100/mo.
- **Hours/account/month (mine):** 2-3 with AI drafting replies, 4-6 without. At $200-250/mo that is about $80-100/hr, and it falls fast if you take $125 accounts.
- **Pool:** Any local business with a profile; no clean free national count. Free proxy: Census CBP establishment counts by NAICS and county, plus OpenStreetMap Overpass queries for a city. I'd expect well above 100k reachable but have no verified number.
- **Free reach channel:** Do a free public audit (unanswered reviews, missing hours) from the public listing, no login needed, and send it to the owner by email or in person; local chamber of commerce groups.
- **Blockers:** Needs profile manager access from the client (their action, not a sign-up for you). Google policy bans fake or incentivised reviews, so reply only. No licence.
- **First dollar (mine):** 1-2 weeks.
- **Demand evidence:** Sold by many agencies and white-label vendors ([Merchynt white-label](https://merchynt.com/post/local-seo-white-label-pricing)), meaning a mature, crowded market. No verified churn data; software at $20-100 caps what you can charge.

## 3. AI phone receptionist setup + monthly care for trades
- **Buyer:** Owner of a 1-10 person HVAC/plumbing/electrical shop that misses calls on jobs.
- **Pays today:** Tools run $20-$50/mo entry, up to a few hundred; human answering $235-$1,500/mo ([AI receptionist cost roundup](https://serviceaitools.pages.dev/ai-receptionist-cost), [Smith.ai HVAC](https://smith.ai/industries/hvac-technicians-answering-service)). Resale claim: agencies charge $400-$700/mo per agent on a $299 plan plus usage ([Acquire listing](https://app.acquire.com/startup/0odoja7t3j-ai-agent-reselling-platform-for-entrepreneurs-to-sell-ai-agents-to-small-businesses)). Setup fee of $500-$1,500 is common in agency pitches but I could not verify it. Direct tool prices at $20-50 undercut you if the owner finds them.
- **Hours/account/month (mine):** 6-8 hrs setup, 1-2 hrs/mo after. About $100-165/hr on setup, $100-200/mo retainer is thin per account.
- **Pool:** NAICS 2382 building equipment contractors; aggregators disagree (about 193k firms per [Mewayz](https://data.mewayz.com/industry/2382), up to about 309k entities per [naics.com](https://www.naics.com/naics-code-description/?code=2382)). Free exact count: Census CBP table for NAICS 23821/23822 on data.census.gov.
- **Free reach channel:** Trade-specific Facebook groups, local supply-house bulletin boards, calling at 7pm to prove the miss (test their own number).
- **Blockers:** Call-recording consent laws (all-party-consent states), disclose AI on calls, FCC treats AI voices as artificial under the TCPA for outbound calls, so keep it inbound-only. Platform costs per minute eat margin. No licence.
- **First dollar (mine):** 2-3 weeks.
- **Demand evidence:** Weak. Vida/SurveyMonkey March 2025 survey of 320 SMBs found 22% use voice agents and 31% plan to within 12-24 months (via [CXM Today](https://cxmtoday.com/?p=33805)). Voice-agency churn is described as a "silent killer" but with no benchmark ([Trillet](https://trillet.ai/blogs/voice-agent-client-retention-strategies)). A Reddit snapshot warns of a race to the bottom. This is the most hyped idea here.

## 4. Newsletter ghostwriting for B2B founders and experts
- **Buyer:** Founder, consultant or coach with an audience but no writing time.
- **Pays today:** $500/mo weekly B2B ([InboxGhosts](https://bestwriting.com/?p=32178)); $1,200 / $2,000 / $2,800 per month tiers ([Natasha Tynes](https://www.natashatynes.com/substack)); newsletter plus LinkedIn $2,497/mo ([Gumroad](https://newsletterwriting.gumroad.com/l/complete)); $3,000-$8,000/mo claimed for senior solo writers ([Sproutworth](https://www.sproutworth.com/newsletter-ghostwriting/)).
- **Hours/account/month (mine):** 4 issues x 2-3 hrs with AI draft plus voice call = 10-14 hrs. $500 = about $40/hr, $2,000 = about $150-200/hr. The price you can hold depends on portfolio, which you do not yet have.
- **Pool:** Not cleanly countable. Free proxy: Substack/Beehiiv/LinkedIn creator search, count of B2B founders with newsletters more than 4 weeks stale. Likely tens of thousands, unverified.
- **Free reach channel:** Publish own sample newsletter, rewrite a stale prospect's last post as a free sample, LinkedIn DMs (manual).
- **Blockers:** None legal; client must approve (disclosure/FTC only if endorsements). Hardest part is trust and voice match.
- **First dollar (mine):** 3-6 weeks (sales cycle plus a trial).
- **Demand evidence:** Many agencies and freelancers list prices, and I saw a mix of Carrd/pages.dev listings that look like hype. Low confidence on the $3k+ tier.

## 5. Podcast show notes + repurposing package
- **Buyer:** Independent podcaster or B2B company podcast.
- **Pays today:** Show notes $60-$100 per episode by length ([Contra listing](https://contra.com/s/ooRY7vcc-podcast-show-notes)); $50-$150 per episode for notes plus social ([search summary](https://weftly-landing.pages.dev/blog/cost-to-post-produce-a-podcast-2026/)). Competing AI tools cost $0.75-$6.50 per episode and Podsqueeze starts at $8.99/mo ([Makerstack](https://makerstack.co/reviews/podsqueeze-review/)).
- **Hours (mine):** 0.3-0.5 hr per episode. A weekly show = $240-400/mo for about 2 hrs, so $150+/hr, but ticket is small and capped at maybe 30-40 shows per solo operator.
- **Pool:** Active podcasts in the hundreds of thousands (unverified). Free count: Apple/Podcast Index API (public) filtered for episodes in the last 30 days.
- **Free reach channel:** Podcast Index to find shows without show notes; email a sample for their latest public episode.
- **Blockers:** None. Real threat: buyers can run the same AI tools for $9/mo, so you are selling convenience, which erodes.
- **First dollar (mine):** 1-2 weeks.
- **Demand evidence:** Priced widely; no proof anyone retains at $60+ once they see the $9 tool.

## 6. Invoice and receipt to spreadsheet/QuickBooks entry (white label to bookkeepers)
- **Buyer:** Small bookkeepers and tax preparers with overflow, contractors with paper receipts.
- **Pays today:** Offshore VAs $6-$10/hr; fully loaded in-house cost about $15/invoice (Quadient stat via vendor blog) ([ImageToTable](https://imagetotable.ai/blog/cost-to-extract-100-invoices-per-month)); tools at $0.07-$0.095 per page ([InvoiceDataExtraction](https://invoicedataextraction.com/)) or $79-$249/mo ([Capterra InvoiceParser](https://www.capterra.com/p/10037704/InvoiceParser-Pro/)).
- **Hours (mine):** Mostly review. Realistic price $0.50-$2 per document, so $20-40 per delivery hour at best. The software sets the price ceiling near zero.
- **Pool:** Same 36M, but the buyers who pay a human are bookkeepers and accountants (tens of thousands; US Census NAICS 5412 count).
- **Free reach channel:** Bookkeeper Facebook groups, QuickBooks ProAdvisor community.
- **Blockers:** Client data handling, NDA; no licence. Accuracy errors have financial consequences.
- **First dollar (mine):** 1-2 weeks. **Verdict:** commodity, skip unless used as a wedge into #1.
- **Demand evidence:** Tooling market is large; paid outsourced demand at human prices is unproven.

## 7. Shopify / marketplace catalog cleanup and listing optimisation
- **Buyer:** Small stores with messy supplier spreadsheets or PDFs.
- **Pays today:** Fiverr/Upwork Shopify data-entry freelancers at about $22/hr and $400-$600 per 2-3 week project ([Fiverr example](https://fiverr.com/samvictor01/do-accurate-shopify-product-data-entry-product-uploads-and-product-listing)). No reliable per-SKU benchmark found.
- **Hours (mine):** Project work; margin around $25-50/hr because offshore competition anchors price.
- **Pool:** 2.5M-3.5M realistic live Shopify stores; counts range from 2.5M to 6.9M by method ([Cropink](https://cropink.com/how-many-shopify-stores-are-there), [ecomm.design](https://ecomm.design/how-many-shopify-stores-are-there/)). Free: StoreLeads public reports.
- **Free reach channel:** Shopify Community forum, r/shopify.
- **Blockers:** Store access via collaborator request. No licence.
- **First dollar (mine):** Days, but low value. **Verdict:** pool is large, price is competed down.

## What I would NOT claim
- No offer here has verified buyer-side revenue. Everything is seller pricing.
- Per-hour figures assume AI cuts time by about 40-60%; that is my estimate and untested.
- Pool numbers for #3 are aggregator figures that disagree by 60%; use Census CBP before relying on them.
- Cheapest validation per offer: post 10 real, manual sample deliverables (audit, notes, newsletter, cleanup quote) to named prospects and count paid replies. Pass signal borrowed from the route doc: a paid reply in the first 100 contacts.

## Fit with the existing route
The existing route is logistics-themed (DOT, freight audit, customs). Ideas 1, 2 and 3 are on a different buyer set (generic local small business and trades), so they would not share a channel with that stack. They serve better as a fast-cash bridge (1-3 weeks) than as part of the $10k plan; at $100-250 per account per month, $10k needs 40-100 accounts, which exceeds the 190 delivery hrs/month for #2 and #1-retainer.
