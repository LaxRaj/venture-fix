# Round 2: new ventures, screened

Sources: `ideas_creator_services.md`, `ideas_client_finding.md`, `ideas_tools.md`, `ideas_new.md`. Screen: `python3 -I analysis/ideas_screen.py`.
Settings: 190 delivery hrs/month, 100 outbound contacts/week, contact-to-paid 0.3% base and 0.1% stress, $57.74/hr bar, 10% monthly churn for cheap services.

**Read this first.** Prices come from vendor blogs and seller pages. Delivery hours, time to first dollar and most pool sizes are the agents' estimates. I set the addressable pools myself (20k-300k) and the screen's pool test passes easily because of that. The screen can kill ideas, not prove them. The meaningful signals here are revenue per hour, accounts needed, and weeks to target.

At $5k the ordering is the same. Newsletter brokerage also fails the pool test at 0.1%.

**Outside the screen (priced per outcome, not flat):**
- **Pay-per-view clipping:** about $1 per 1,000 views after a 20-30% cut, shared budgets. Skip as a business.
- **Thumbnails:** $5-20 each. Skip.
- **Template and prompt packs:** fast first dollar, but one-off revenue and discovery is luck.
- **Niche lead-list pipeline:** not a product. Use it internally for the logistics route.

## What this changes
- **Few-account, higher-ticket offers win.** At 0.3%, every offer needing 10+ accounts takes 33+ weeks to reach $3k by cold outreach. The ones needing 2-4 accounts take 6-13 weeks.
- **Creator services get a faster first dollar than the logistics stack** (agents estimate 1-3 weeks, with sample-led outreach). They face heavier price pressure from offshore editors and AI tools. The logistics stack has thinner supply.
- **Shared channel.** Short-form repurposing, long-form editing, podcast editing and newsletter ghostwriting all sell to creators and podcasters, so one list and one sample-first pitch serves all four.

## Route update
Do not replace the logistics stack. Add a second, creator-facing stack as the faster first-dollar path and run both against the same hour budget:
1. **Short-form repurposing**, pitched with a free sample clip, for creators with 10K+ subscribers. First dollar target: weeks 1-3.
2. **Upsell to long-form editing or newsletter ghostwriting** for the accounts that stay. These are the high-ticket, few-account offers.
3. **Appointment setting for one metro of roofers/HVAC** only after written-consent (TCPA) rules are handled. It is the best $/hr among the client-finding offers (about $80).
Skip: sponsor brokerage, bookkeeping retainer, GBP management, AI receptionist, tools and pay-per-view clipping.

## Cheapest real-world tests (before building anything)
| Offer | Test | Pass signal |
|---|---|---|
| Short-form repurposing | Make 3 sample shorts from public long videos by 30 creators in one niche; offer 15 shorts for $400-750/mo | 1 paid in 3 weeks |
| Long-form editing | Offer the same creators one free edit of an old video | 1 retainer request |
| Newsletter ghostwriting | Offer 20 newsletter owners one free issue draft | 1 paid trial |
| Appointment setting | Ask 20 roofers/HVAC firms in one metro whether they would pay $150 per kept appointment | 1 signed pilot |

## Still unknown

- **Whether the $750/month price holds** against offshore editors at $400-1,500/month.
- **Real service hours.** Time the first three accounts.
- **Pool sizes** for every row (my guesses).
- **Demand proof:** no buyer-side evidence of payment was found for any new offer. Reddit and Upwork were not reachable.
